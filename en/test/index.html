Closed test on Google Play

Test Zappy TV and get 6 months free.

Zappy TV is a light player for Android TV, Google TV and Android phones. It has no channels: you add the M3U or Xtream playlist from the provider you already pay for. To publish on Google Play, I need people testing for 14 days.

Your reward

Everyone who stays the 14 days and fills the form gets 6 months of the full version, on up to 5 devices.

Who can join

  • An Android phone (7.0 or newer), or a TV with Android TV or Google TV
  • A Google account (the same one you use on the Play Store)
  • Fire TV doesn't count yet: it installs from Amazon, and this test is on Google Play

How to join

It takes about 3 minutes. Follow the steps in order, always with the same Google account.

  1. 1

    Step 1: Join the testers group

    Open the group and tap Join group. This is what unlocks your access to the test.

    Join the group
  2. 2

    Step 2: Opt in to the test

    Tap the button to become a tester. If it says the app is not available, check step 1 and try again in a few minutes.

    Opt in
  3. 3

    Step 3: Install from Google Play

    On your phone, tap Install. During the test the app doesn't show up in Play search, so use this button.

    Open on Google Play
  4. 4

    Step 4: Use it for 14 days and tell me what you think

    Keep the app installed and use it when you can. At the end, fill the form (2 minutes) and leave your email to get the 6 months.

    Fill the form

Installing on a TV?

Open the step 3 button on a computer or phone, sign in with the same Google account as the TV, tap Install and pick your TV from the device list. Zappy TV shows up on the TV in a few minutes.

Don't uninstall the app or leave the group before the 14 days. Google only counts people who stay in the test.

Questions

Does the app include channels?

No. Zappy TV is a player. To test it, use the M3U playlist or Xtream login from the provider you already pay for.

The link says the app is not available

Usually you haven't joined the group yet (step 1) or you're on a different account. Make sure the group account is the same as your Play Store account and wait a few minutes.

I can't find the app in Play search

That's normal during the test. Use the step 3 button, which goes straight to the app page.

Will I pay anything?

No. The app is free to download and the test costs nothing. The 6 months of the full version are granted to the email you leave in the form, after the test.

Can I leave the test?

Yes, any time, from the step 2 link. Just remember the reward is for people who stay the 14 days.

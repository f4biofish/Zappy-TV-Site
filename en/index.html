Frequently asked questions

Does the app come with channels?

No. Zappy TV is a player: you use the M3U playlist or Xtream login provided by your provider.

Which playlist formats are supported?

M3U playlists (by link or file) and Xtream server logins (server, username and password). The program guide (XMLTV) is read too.

Which devices does it work on?

Android TV and Google TV, Fire TV, phones with Android 7.0 or newer and computers with Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit).

Is the app free?

You can try everything for free for 7 days. After that, the app stays free with some features limited (they show a padlock). The full version unlocks everything on up to 5 devices. Details in the Terms of use.

Windows warned about an "unknown publisher". Is it safe?

The installer doesn't have a digital signature yet, so Windows shows this warning. Click "More info" and then "Run anyway". To check the file, compare the SHA-256 code shown on the Windows card with the result of the Get-FileHash ZappyTV-windows.msi command in PowerShell.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, under My license, or on the Delete account page.

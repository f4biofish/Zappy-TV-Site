Start on the TV, keep going on your phone.

Sync keeps your favorites, the point where you stopped each movie or episode and your recent channels the same on every device on your account. It starts off, and you choose what's included.

What syncs

  • Favorites: channels, movies and series, in the same order
  • Continue watching: movies and episodes resume at the same point
  • Recent channels: the channels watched most recently
  • What you remove on one device is removed on the others too

What you need

  • The full version, activated with your account on the devices
  • The same playlist from the same provider on the devices that will sync
  • Zappy TV version 0.1.6 or newer
  • In the free version, the option shows a padlock

How to turn it on

Do this on each device that will take part. Every device starts with sync off.

  1. 1

    Step 1: Open Sync

    In the app, go to Settings > Sync. Under This device's group you'll see Off.

  2. 2

    Step 2: Create a group or pick one

    Select Create group and give it a name, such as Family. If the group already exists, use Choose a group. An account can have up to 5 groups.

  3. 3

    Step 3: Turn on what you want to sync

    Choose from Favorites, Continue watching and Recent channels. Each device decides what it shares.

  4. 4

    Step 4: Repeat on your other devices

    On the TV, phone or PC, open the same screen and pick the same group. Done: what you mark on one shows up on the others.

One group per person

Devices in a group share with each other, and different groups never mix. For example, create Family for the living room and your phone, and Kids for the bedroom TV. Under Account groups you can see how many devices are in each group and rename or delete it.

When it syncs

  • When you open the app
  • When you return to the app after 1 hour
  • About 10 seconds after a change, like adding a favorite
  • Your place in a movie or episode, 1 minute after you stop watching
  • Any time, with the Sync now button

What doesn't sync

  • Your playlists and your provider login details
  • Parental controls: hidden and locked channels and the PIN
  • Items from different playlists: every device needs the same playlist

Privacy

The Zappy TV server only stores scrambled codes that identify each item. It never receives playlist addresses, usernames, passwords or channel names. The data is removed from the server when you delete the group or your account, or after 90 days without the full version. More details in the Privacy policy.

Questions

A favorite didn't show up on my other device

Check that both devices are in the same group, with Favorites turned on, and use the same playlist from the same provider. Then select Sync now on both.

Does deleting a group delete my favorites?

No. The devices in the group just stop syncing and keep everything they already had. What was stored on the server for that group is deleted.

How do I turn sync off?

In Settings > Sync, choose Off as this device's group. The other devices stay in the group.

Does it work in the free version?

No. Sync is part of the full version. In the free version the option shows a padlock.

Does it need to run all the time?

No. The app runs nothing in the background: it syncs while it's open, at the moments listed above.
